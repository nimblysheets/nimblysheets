# Nimbly Sheets

Editable Excel templates for freelancers, autónomos and small sellers. Start with a free `.xlsx` workbook, then compare the exact capacity and contents of a related full edition when one is available.

## Free workbooks

[Download the multilingual free workbook pack](https://github.com/nimblysheets/free-spreadsheet-templates/releases/latest) — eight files for English, Spanish, Mexico and Brazil. The files contain no macros and can be opened in Microsoft Excel or imported into Google Sheets.

## Scope

Nimbly Sheets workbooks organize information entered by the user and apply visible arithmetic. They do not connect to accounts or platforms, verify external information, provide tax, legal, accounting, financial, regulatory, commercial or other professional advice, determine obligations or compliance, or establish suitability, benefit or future results.
