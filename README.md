Editable Excel templates for freelancers, autónomos and small sellers. Start with a free `.xlsx` workbook, then compare the exact capacity and contents of a related full edition when one is available.

## Free Excel template hub

**[Browse and directly download eight free workbooks](https://nimblysheets.github.io/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=free_resource_hub)** — English, Spanish, Mexico and Brazil editions for bookkeeping, income and expenses, invoice tracking, freelance rates and reseller inventory.

[Download the complete multilingual pack](https://github.com/nimblysheets/free-spreadsheet-templates/releases/download/v1.1.0/Nimbly-Sheets-Free-Excel-Templates-v1.1.0.zip) — eight files plus localized instructions. The files contain no macros and can be opened in Microsoft Excel or imported into Google Sheets; some formatting or features may vary by application or version.
